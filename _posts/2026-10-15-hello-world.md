Welcome. This is where I'll publish writeups from my day-to-day work in offensive
security — the vulnerabilities I find, the techniques I use, and the AI-assisted
workflows I've built to move faster.

## What to expect

- **Writeups** of interesting bugs and full attack chains.
- **Techniques** for web apps, APIs, thick clients, and Active Directory.
- **Notes** on automating recon, source review, and triage with AI models.
- **Red team** stories and assumed-breach scenarios.

I write to share and to sharpen my own thinking. If something helps you, or if I
got something wrong, reach out — I'd love to hear from you.

— Yogender
